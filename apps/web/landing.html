Before you ask

A few practical questions.

Where is my data?

Stored with Cloudflare in the Oceania region, backed up nightly.

Can I get my data out?

Yes: one export file, any time.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your account becomes read-only until you choose a plan. Nothing is deleted.

Do parents need an app?

No. The parent view works in their phone’s browser. Parents sign in with the email their centre has for them.

Is it safe for children’s data?

We keep only what a feature needs and delete what’s no longer needed. Read the privacy policy.
